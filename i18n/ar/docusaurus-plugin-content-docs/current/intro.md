# Welcome to Wordiy llll

**Professional Translation Management Made Simple**

Wordiy is a comprehensive translation management platform designed for teams and organizations to collaborate seamlessly on translations. Whether you're managing a small project or a global product, Wordiy simplifies every step of the translation workflow.

You can invite team members and assign roles to control permissions. [Learn more →](/team/invite-members)
