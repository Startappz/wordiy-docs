# Create Your Wordiy Account

Get started with Wordiy in just a few minutes.

## Sign Up Steps

1. Go to [wordiy.dev](https://wordiy.dev)
2. Click **Sign Up** in the top navigation
3. Enter your email address
4. Create a secure password
5. Click **Create Account**
6. Check your email and verify your account

## Sign Up Options

### Email & Password
Traditional email signup with a password.

### Social Login
You can also sign up using:
- Google
- GitHub
- Other OAuth providers

## What Happens Next?

After signing up, you'll be guided through:
1. Creating your first organization
2. Choosing a plan
3. Creating your first project

## Troubleshooting

**Already have an account?**
[Log in here](https://wordiy.dev/login)

**Didn't receive verification email?**
Check your spam folder or contact support.
