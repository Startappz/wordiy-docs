# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Wordiy?
Wordiy is a translation management platform that helps teams collaborate on translating applications.

### How much does Wordiy cost?
We offer free and paid plans. See [Pricing](https://wordiy.dev/pricing) for details.

### Do you offer a free trial?
Yes! Start with our free plan with no credit card required.

## Projects & Strings

### How do I upload my strings?
See [Upload Strings](docs/translations/upload-strings.md) for step-by-step instructions.

### Can I edit strings after uploading?
Yes, you can add, edit, and delete strings anytime.

### What file formats do you support?
We support JSON, YAML, CSV, and more. See the export documentation for details.

## Team & Permissions

### How do I invite team members?
See [Invite Members](docs/team/invite-members.md) for detailed steps.

### What are the different roles?
- **Translator** — Can translate strings
- **Reviewer** — Can review and approve translations
- **Manager** — Can manage team and project settings
- **Owner** — Full access to the organization

## Machine Translation

### Can Wordiy automatically translate my strings?
Yes! Use our machine translation feature. See [Machine Translation](docs/ai/machine-translation.md).

### Which providers do you support?
- Google Translate
- DeepL
- and more

## Billing & Subscription

### Can I change my plan?
Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. See [Subscription](docs/billing/subscription.md).

### Do you offer annual discounts?
Contact our sales team for volume pricing.
