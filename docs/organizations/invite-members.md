# Invite Team Members

Build your team by inviting members to your organization.

## Before You Start

You need:

- **Owner or Admin** role in the organization
- Team member's **email address**
- Know which **organization role** they should have (Owner, Admin, or Member)

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Navigate to Members Page

From your organization dashboard:

1. Click on your **Organization** name at the top
2. Go to **Settings** → **Members**
3. You'll see the Members page with your current team and an **"Invite Member"** button

![Members Page](/img/screenshots/organizations-members.png)

### 2. Click "Invite Member" Button

On the Members page, click the **"Invite Member"** button to open the invitation form.

### 3. Enter Email Address

In the invitation form:

- Enter the team member's email address in the **"Email Address"** field
- You can invite multiple people at once by separating email addresses with commas

![Invite Form](/img/screenshots/organizations-invite-form.png)

### 4. Select Organization Role

Choose the appropriate organization-level role:

- **Owner** — Full organization control (invite only if you trust them completely)
- **Admin** — Can manage team members and projects
- **Member** — Limited access, project-level permissions assigned separately

Learn more about [roles and permissions](/docs/organizations/manage-roles).

![Complete Invitation Form](/img/screenshots/organizations-invite-form.png)

### 5. (Optional) Assign Project Roles

If inviting as a Member, you can assign them specific roles in projects:

- **Translator** — Can create and edit translations
- **Reviewer** — Can review and approve translations
- **Viewer** — Read-only access

You can also assign project roles after they join.

### 6. Add Personal Message (Optional)

Include a personal message to welcome the team member:

- Explain why you're inviting them
- Share relevant links or context
- Set expectations for the role

Example:

> "Hi Sarah! We're using Wordiy for our translation workflow. I've invited you to our organization to help with translations. Looking forward to collaborating!"

### 7. Send Invitation

Click **"Send Invitation"**

The team member will receive an email with:

- Your organization name
- Their assigned organization role
- A link to join Wordiy
- Your personal message (if included)
- Instructions on how to accept

## What Happens Next

### For New Wordiy Users

1. They receive invitation email
2. Click link in email
3. Create Wordiy account
4. Accept invitation
5. Get access to your organization

### For Existing Wordiy Users

1. They receive invitation email
2. Click link or go to account settings
3. Accept invitation in "Pending Invitations"
4. Immediately get access to organization

## Managing Invitations

### View Pending Invitations

Go to **Organization** → **Members** → **Pending Invitations**

You'll see:

- Invitee email
- Assigned organization role
- Date invited
- Status (pending, accepted, expired)

### Resend Invitation

If someone didn't receive the email:

1. Find invitation in Pending list
2. Click **"Resend"**
3. They'll get another copy

### Cancel Invitation

Before they accept:

1. Find invitation in Pending list
2. Click **"Cancel"**
3. They won't be able to accept anymore

### Invitation Expiration

Invitations expire after **30 days**

To add them later:

- Send a new invitation
- They can accept if they join Wordiy before expiration

## Assigning Project Roles After Joining

Once a Member joins your organization, assign them specific project roles:

1. Go to **Members**
2. Find the member
3. In the projects table, select their role (Translator, Reviewer, Viewer, or None)
4. Save changes

## Removing Members

If someone should no longer have access:

1. Go to **Members**
2. Find the member
3. Click **"Remove"**
4. Confirm removal

They'll immediately lose access to:

- All projects in organization
- Organization settings
- Team data

**Note:** Their translations are preserved for audit purposes.

## Inviting Existing Team Members

If someone is already in another organization:

1. Send them an invitation
2. They'll see it in their pending invitations
3. They can accept to join your organization
4. They'll have access to both organizations

## Best Practices

- **Clear roles** — Make sure invitees understand their role
- **Security** — Only assign Owner role to trusted administrators
- **Gradual access** — Start with Member role, assign specific projects as needed
- **Early onboarding** — Send welcome materials with invitation
- **Regular reviews** — Check team member status and roles monthly
- **Communication** — Let team know who's joining

## Troubleshooting

### Invitation Not Received

- Check spam/junk folder
- Verify email address is correct
- Resend invitation

### Member Can't Accept

- Check if they have a Wordiy account
- Verify invitation hasn't expired
- Resend if needed

### Need to Change Role

- Update their organization role in the Members list
- For project-specific roles, update the project role assignment
