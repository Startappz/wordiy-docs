# Create an Organization

Learn how to create a new organization in Wordiy.

## Steps to Create an Organization

### 1. Sign Up or Log In

If you don't have a Wordiy account yet:
- Visit [wordiy.dev](https://wordiy.dev)
- Click "Sign Up"
- Choose sign up method (email or OAuth)
- Create your account

### 2. Navigate to Organization Creation

After logging in:
1. Click on your profile icon in the top-right corner
2. Select "Create Organization"
3. Or go directly to the organizations page

### 3. Enter Organization Details

Fill in the following information:

- **Organization Name** — Your company or team name
- **Organization Slug** — URL-friendly identifier (auto-generated from name)
- **Website** (optional) — Your company website
- **Description** (optional) — Brief description of your organization

![Create Organization Form](/img/screenshots/organizations-create.png)

### 4. Verify Email

You'll receive a verification email. Click the link to confirm your organization.

### 5. Start Using Your Organization

Once verified:
- You're automatically set as the **Owner**
- Your organization dashboard is ready
- You can start creating projects and inviting members

## Organization Naming Tips

- **Use clear, descriptive names** — "Acme Corp Translations" is better than "Project X"
- **Include context** — If you have multiple organizations, include department names
- **Keep it concise** — Shorter names are easier to type and remember

## What's Next?

After creating your organization:

1. **Invite team members** — Add team members as Admin or Member role
2. **Create your first project** — Set up a project for your application
3. **Assign project roles** — Assign Translator, Reviewer, or Viewer roles within projects
4. **Configure settings** — Set up integrations and preferences
5. **Upload strings** — Start adding your translatable content

## Organization Limits

Your plan determines:
- Maximum number of organizations
- Maximum projects per organization
- Maximum team members
- Storage and bandwidth limits

See [Billing & Pricing](/docs/organizations/billing) for more details.
